Every engagement is shaped around the organization’s needs, but most of my work falls into five areas.

01

Executive Advisory

A trusted partner for the decisions you cannot make in isolation.

Executive leadership can be lonely, particularly when an organization is growing, changing, or facing decisions with no obvious answer. I provide an experienced, outside perspective while staying close enough to understand the organization and its context.

This may include

  • Working through complex organizational decisions
  • Clarifying priorities and tradeoffs
  • Preparing for difficult board or team conversations
  • Navigating executive and leadership dynamics
  • Moving from reaction to more intentional leadership
  • Holding momentum on critical priorities

Best suited for

CEOs and executive directors who want an ongoing strategic thought partner.

02

Fractional Leadership

Experienced executive capacity without adding a full-time position.

I step into the organization as an embedded senior leader to help clarify direction, strengthen organizational infrastructure, develop internal capacity, and move priority initiatives forward.

This may include

  • Translating strategy into an operating plan
  • Strengthening leadership and organizational accountability
  • Clarifying roles, responsibilities, and decision-making
  • Improving coordination across teams and functions
  • Leading high-priority initiatives that cross organizational boundaries

Best suited for

Organizations experiencing growth, transition, or executive capacity constraints.

03

Strategic and Operational Planning

A clear direction and a practical plan to move it forward.

I guide organizations through strategic planning processes that clarify where they are, where they want to go, and the choices required to get there, then help translate that direction into priorities, responsibilities, and operational plans that support meaningful execution.

This may include

  • Comprehensive strategic planning
  • Strategy refreshes and organizational assessments
  • Stakeholder engagement and facilitated planning sessions
  • Strategic priorities, goals, and measures of progress
  • Annual and multi-year operational planning

Best suited for

Organizations developing a new strategy, responding to significant change, or seeking a clearer connection between long-term direction and day-to-day work.

04

Nonprofit Mergers and Strategic Partnerships

Thoughtful guidance when preserving the mission may mean reimagining the organization.

Exploring a merger, strategic partnership, or dissolution requires more than legal and financial analysis. These decisions also involve mission, people, power, governance, culture, funding, and organizational identity.

I help leaders and boards explore their options, identify and assess potential partners, and determine the path that will best protect the mission and the communities they serve. When a direction is chosen, I provide grounded support to move the organization through it with clarity, care, and integrity.

This may include

  • Organizational readiness and options assessment
  • Partner exploration and identification
  • Merger and partnership discernment
  • Board and leadership decision-making
  • Mission, culture, governance, and stakeholder considerations
  • Responsible dissolution and wind-down

Best suited for

Organizations considering whether a merger, partnership, or responsible dissolution could strengthen or preserve their mission.

05

Workshops and Retreats

The conversations that matter, designed to lead somewhere.

I facilitate focused experiences that allow teams and boards to step away from day-to-day demands, engage honestly, and build shared direction.

Sessions may focus on

  • Strategic priorities
  • Board and staff alignment
  • Organizational roles and decision-making
  • Leadership team effectiveness
  • Scenario planning
  • Strategy implementation
  • Growth and organizational readiness
  • Navigating a significant transition

Every engagement

Each engagement includes thoughtful preparation, facilitation, and clear synthesis of decisions and next steps.
