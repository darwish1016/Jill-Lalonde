Nonprofits are powered by passion and commitment.

They also need strong leadership, sound operations, and effective systems.

For nearly two decades, I have worked alongside mission-driven leaders and teams to build stronger organizations and expand their impact. My experience spans global development, nonprofit leadership, organizational strategy, and governance, with organizations working across the United States, Africa, Asia, the Middle East, and Central America.

I spent a decade as an Executive Director, leading through significant growth and transition. That experience taught me that while nonprofits are powered by passion and commitment, they also need strong leadership, sound operations, and effective systems to advance their missions and sustain their impact.

Throughout my career, I have also been deeply engaged in community-led development and in helping organizations center proximate voices in the decisions that shape their work. Alongside my consulting work, I am an Advisor and Adjunct Lecturer in Columbia University’s MS Nonprofit Management program.

These experiences allow me to understand organizations from multiple perspectives, as an executive responsible for results, an advisor helping leaders see the bigger picture, and an educator exploring what effective and lasting impact requires. I bring that perspective to every partnership, helping leaders make sense of complexity, clarify what their organization needs, and create a practical path forward.

The goal is greater clarity, stronger decisions, and meaningful progress.

A calm, honest partner in complicated moments.

I create space for leaders and teams to step out of reaction mode, speak candidly, and think more clearly about what their organization needs.

My approach is curious, direct, grounded, and practical. I ask questions that uncover what is really happening, help people name what may have gone unspoken, and bring enough structure to move the conversation toward decisions.

I am comfortable with ambiguity, but I do not leave organizations there. The goal is not simply a thoughtful conversation or an elegant strategy. It is greater clarity, stronger decisions, and meaningful progress.

What I believe.

  • Great missions deserve equally strong organizations.
  • Strategy without execution is only aspiration.
  • Growth without infrastructure creates fragility.
  • Strong governance enables leadership; it should not replace it.
  • The best organizational solutions are built with people, not imposed on them.
